KEY DISCOVERY — ClassificationSchema API:
  SketchUp has NATIVE IFC classification support via:
    Sketchup::ClassificationSchema + Sketchup::Classifications
  This means we do NOT need an IFC exporter library for Tier 6.
  Strategy:
    Step 1: Assign IFC 2x3 classifications in our Ruby script:
      IfcColumn, IfcBeam, IfcFooting, IfcMember
    Step 2: Use SketchUp's built-in File > Export > IFC
    Step 3: All attributes and classification data exports automatically
  This is the shortest path to LOD350 IFC export.
